About the project

Artificial intelligence is increasingly being used in creative fields such as filmmaking, graphic design, photography, music, and writing. While these tools can make parts of the creative process faster and more accessible, they also raise questions about originality, authorship, creative ownership, and the future of creative jobs.

This project uses Tracery-style grammar rules to generate statements connecting different forms of AI with specific creative practices and the concerns they create. By focusing on the relationship between technology and human creativity, the project explores how AI is changing the way creative work is produced and understood.

As generative AI becomes more common in media industries, understanding its effects on human creators will become increasingly important.
